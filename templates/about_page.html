About

I'm Ryan, though you'll usually find me online as Ixirsii. I'm a software engineer with more than eight years of experience building web applications, APIs, and distributed systems. Most of my professional work has been in Java and Kotlin, building backend services on top of SQL and NoSQL databases, but I enjoy learning new technologies and have recently been spending more of my free time writing Rust and experimenting with FreeBSD, self-hosted infrastructure, and home networking. I like understanding how systems fit together, from application code all the way down to the operating system and hardware.

Outside of work, I'm an avid reader with a particular love for fantasy novels, a lifelong gamer, and a music enthusiast. I played drums through high school and college and still hope to pick up guitar someday. I contribute to open source software and occasionally write essays defending positions that should never have escaped the notes app. When I'm not reading or tinkering with a homelab project, you'll probably find me playing games with friends or revisiting the Mass Effect trilogy for the umpteenth time.

This blog is where I write about software, operating systems, books, and confidently held opinions that may or may not survive contact with reality.

Contributors who help improve this site, including through responsible security disclosures, are recognized on the Hall of Fame.
